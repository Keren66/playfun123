Neon Shrine

Neon Shrine is a card RPG about the shrines hidden inside Tokyo's crossings, and the spirits who now run them like small businesses. You are the ward's newest contractor.

Build a deck out of the city's memories: a train announcement that stops time, a vending machine that grants one wish, a convenience-store receipt that lists your future. Coming to iOS and Android in 2026.

Meet the contractors

Three people keeping one ward lit until morning.

Core gameplay

Cards are memories. The city is the deck.

A deck from the city

Cards are memories, not monsters. Every location you cleanse adds its own small, strange card to your pool.

Ward contract

Each season you sign with one shrine. It pays, it demands, and it decides which cards your deck can hold.

Night clock

Fights run from dusk to dawn. The later it gets, the stronger your cards get — and the worse the ward holds.

FAQs

When is Neon Shrine out?

2026 for iOS and Android. We will announce a month, then a date, once the closed test is finished — not before.

How do I get into the test?

Sign-ups go to our mailing list first, then to everyone. Write to contact@playfun123.com and we will add you.

Is it a sequel to your other games?

No. It shares our art team and nothing else.

Will it be free to play?

Yes, with one paid story chapter per season. No energy timers, no ads.

Which regions launch first?

Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia and Japan at launch; everywhere else about two months later.
