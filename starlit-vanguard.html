STARLIT VANGUARD

Tactical mecha RPG

Starlit Vanguard

Starlit Vanguard is a turn-based tactics RPG about a squadron of pilots holding a star system together after its sun began to fail. You command three frames at a time, and every fight is decided by where you stand — not by how long you grind.

Build a squad from twenty pilots, refit their frames between sorties, and carry the consequences of each mission into the next chapter.

Download on theApp Store Get it onGoogle Play

Meet the squad

Three pilots, one carrier, and no reinforcements coming.

Core gameplay

Move, break line of sight, and win the turn before the shooting starts.

Positioning

Cover, elevation and facing decide the fight. Break line of sight and the enemy spends its whole turn walking to you.

Frame loadout

Every frame carries three hardpoints. Swap weapons between sorties instead of unlocking a bigger number.

Command link

Spend your action to hand it to a squadmate. Chain three commands in one turn and the last one lands twice as hard.

Live events

A new operation every two weeks, and a season that changes the map.

FAQs

Is Starlit Vanguard free to play?

Yes. Campaign chapters, story missions and ranked are free. Frames, weapon modules and cosmetics are sold in the store, and no campaign mission requires them.

Do I need an internet connection?

Campaign chapters download once and then play offline. Ranked, co-op raids and live events need a connection.

Which platforms does it run on?

iOS 15 and later, Android 9 and later, and Windows through Steam. One account carries progress across all three.

How big is the download?

About 3.2 GB on mobile once the art packs are in; the base install is 1.1 GB.

Can I replay story missions?

Every cleared chapter replays at your current squad level, or you can lock the squad back to its original state for the harder version.
