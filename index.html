About us

We make anime games

Play Fun is a Singapore studio that develops and publishes anime games for mobile and PC. We started in August 2023 with one rule: the art comes first, and then the game has to earn it.

We build our own titles end to end — character design, combat, seasonal content — and we publish partner studios across Southeast Asia who want their worlds in front of a global audience.

Develop

Original titles from first sketch to global launch, art direction included.

Publish

Store setup, localisation and user acquisition for studios that would rather stay in the editor.

Live ops

Seasons, events and balance updates that keep a game worth opening in year three.
