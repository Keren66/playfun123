Petal & Blade

Petal & Blade is a hand-painted action RPG set in an Edo that has been burning for eleven years. You play a swordswoman working off a debt to a castle that no longer exists, one duel at a time.

Combat runs on parry timing rather than combo lists. Read the blade, answer it, and the fight ends in two cuts. Read it wrong and it ends in one.

The three who ride with her

A sword, a ledger, and a man the rain follows.

Core gameplay

One blade, one window, one answer.

Parry windows

Every enemy attack has a tell. Answer inside the window and the fight opens. Miss it and you lose the exchange, not the level.

Momentum

Clean parries fill a gauge that changes your stance. Spend it on a finisher, or hold it to keep the enemy's guard broken.

The burning map

Villages you save keep their merchants, side stories and smith. Villages you pass are gone from the map for good.

Seasonal duels

The world moves whether or not you are in it.

FAQs

Is there a difficulty setting?

Three of them: Story (parry windows relaxed, duels cannot be failed), Blade (the intended tuning), and Iron (one hit, no retries inside a chapter). You can change it between chapters.

Will Petal & Blade come to PC?

A Steam build is in testing. Mobile ships first; we will announce the PC date when it is fixed, not before.

Does it need a controller?

No. Touch controls are the primary scheme and were tuned first. Bluetooth controllers work on both platforms.

How long is the story?

About twelve hours for the main line, twenty for a completionist run.

Are there in-app purchases?

One expansion chapter a year, sold once. No energy, no gacha, no ads.
